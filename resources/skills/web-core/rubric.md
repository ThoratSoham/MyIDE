# Web Core rubric

The critic scores each generated site from 1 to 10 on every criterion below.
Scores are whole numbers; the overall score is the mean of the ten criteria.

1. **Visual hierarchy** — one clear focal point per section, headings ranked
   by size and weight, supporting text subordinate to the primary message.
2. **Spacing and rhythm** — consistent spacing steps, balanced whitespace,
   sections that breathe without feeling empty.
3. **Colour and contrast** — palette used with intent, text readable against
   its real background, accent reserved for emphasis and calls to action.
4. **Typography** — sensible heading and body pairing, readable measure,
   consistent scaling, no orphaned or oversized text.
5. **Icons and imagery** — decorative icons marked as such, gradient media
   blocks that read as intentional, nothing that looks like a broken image.
6. **Motion quality** — animation that supports the content, respects reduced
   motion, and never leaves content invisible.
7. **Content quality** — specific and concrete copy, no filler or placeholder
   language, every section carrying real information.
8. **Responsiveness** — no horizontal overflow, sensible reflow, tap targets
   and body text sized correctly on small screens.
9. **Consistency** — the same tokens, spacing, and components applied
   throughout, no one-off styling.
10. **Overall polish** — the whole page feels finished, coherent, and ready
    to share.

**Pass mark:** verifier has no errors and overall score >= 8.0.
